Canonical design
A handheld single-shot grenade launcher cast predominantly from genuinely clear glass. Its main silhouette is one continuous, coaxial glass cannon running lengthwise from compact rear breech to large circular muzzle. The rear-to-middle glass body swells smoothly upward and downward into an integrated bottle-belly chamber, then narrows naturally into the forward tube and thick large-caliber muzzle lip. The swell is part of the barrel itself, never a separate jar, pot, sphere, bottle, or hanging container.

The main pistol grip and forward support grip are also thick molded transparent-glass castings. Both use broad sturdy forms, simple finger grooves, curved refractive edges, and a small cyan/prismatic triangular point. Metal is limited to two thin structural reinforcement rings, the compact rear breech/trigger mechanism, and minimal connectors. The interior stays uncluttered with one simple coaxial warm-white/cyan filament.

Material priority
Almost colorless, very high-transparency true glass. Opposite-side contours and the inner bore remain visible through broad faces. Strong refraction and cyan/prismatic color appear mainly at physically thick rims, edges, bevels, and the small grip points. No opaque turquoise/blue plastic, frosted glass, milky body, or black rubber handles.

Shared constraints
Strict orthographic projection, one view per PNG, centered and fully unclipped. Robust connected low-complexity forms suitable for T2 reconstruction. Genuine alpha=0 background. No checkerboard pixels, floor, shadow, environment, stock, scope, magazine, drum, extra barrels, separate vessel, excessive metal bands, fine greebles, hands, characters, text, labels, logos, smoke, particles, floating parts, or hair-thin geometry.
