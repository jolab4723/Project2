# Dockbreaker Meshy 7 raw-geometry QA

Run this gate on the untextured GLB before remesh, texturing, Blender correction, or Unity import.

## Required views

- left and right orthographic side
- muzzle and stock axial views
- top and bottom
- at least two isometric views
- close views of the open muzzle, trigger grip, foregrip, and both anchor-stock cutouts

## Immediate pass requirements

- The stock, receiver, barrel, open muzzle, trigger grip, and foregrip form one coherent weapon.
- The anchor is unmistakable in both side views and is physically integrated into the stock.
- Both stock cutouts remain open; neither becomes a thin torn sheet or an unrelated floating hook.
- The muzzle has exactly one centered open bore with a continuous rim and visible depth.
- The four restrained muzzle prongs remain attached to the housing and do not cross the bore.
- The trigger grip and foregrip remain separate, clean one-hand contact surfaces.
- The full silhouette appears on the side axis, not folded onto the top or bottom axis.

## Immediate rejection

- capped, painted, duplicated, or depthless muzzle
- stock or muzzle prong islands that are visibly detached
- anchor openings filled, collapsed, or converted to extra barrels
- barrel, receiver, or stock discontinuity
- 2.5D fold, wrong-axis reconstruction, or unrelated internal mass
- a grip that requires rebuilding the receiver or moving the full weapon geometry

Local repair is allowed only for a small seam, one small decorative plate, or a shallow local muzzle cleanup while the approved silhouette and both hand-contact regions remain intact.
