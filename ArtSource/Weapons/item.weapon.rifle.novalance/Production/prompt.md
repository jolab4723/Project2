# Novalance — Image Prompt Record

`item.weapon.rifle.novalance` is a fictional precision plasma rifle built for the Gunner silhouette.

## Stable design tokens

- Open triangular white composite rear frame with black butt pad
- Slim white/graphite monolithic body with sparse panel density
- Circular regulator with a segmented deep-cobalt inset ring
- Connected upper energy spine made from hard-edged cobalt inset plates under a continuous opaque white/black truss cap
- Closed spearhead emitter cage, recessed cobalt plates and a solid white spear tip
- Needle-thin activation lever and short bracketed horizontal support grip

## Energy boundary contract

All cobalt areas are opaque, non-luminous physical ceramic/anodized-metal inserts. They remain inside opaque white/black housings. No blue surface is intended as aura geometry, and the images contain no visible glow, bloom, haze, translucent energy shell or floating energy component.

## View contract

Four independent genuine-alpha orthographic views are prepared in positional API order `front,left,back,right`. FRONT shows the solid white tip and recessed plates rather than a large oval core; BACK is the direct butt-pad projection.
