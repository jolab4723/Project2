Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: four-view orthographic 3D weapon modeling reference
Item ID: item.weapon.shotgun.sulbing

Design anchor
- Rare ice shotgun whose identity reads immediately as Korean bingsu / snowflake shaved ice, not merely a generic frost gun.
- Serious, short and chunky combat shotgun: short thick barrel, solid shoulder stock, clear right-hand pistol grip, and broad connected front-hand pump.
- Only three dominant decorative forms: an armored rounded bingsu-bowl receiver, one connected three-tier snowflake-ice mound, and one restrained inset strawberry-red/mango-gold/mint syrup accent band.
- Frosted white, ice blue, silver, and deep navy material zones; food cues are structural rather than loose props.
- One coherent, manufacturable, Meshy-friendly connected silhouette with controlled low-to-medium complexity.
- No literal detachable bowl, spoon, loose fruit/toppings, floating snow, fog, loose crystals, shards, icicles, charms, wires, or small scattered decoration.

View contract
- left.png: true orthographic left elevation, stock left / muzzle right.
- right.png: true orthographic opposite side, muzzle left / stock right.
- front.png: true orthographic muzzle view centered on the short thick barrel, with the smaller lower tube visible.
- rear.png: true orthographic stock view centered on the navy butt pad, with the wider bowl receiver and tiered ice mound logically visible behind it.
- Identical major geometry, proportions, colors, material zones, and bingsu identity across all views.
- Exactly one centered, fully visible weapon; no character, hands, text, label, logo, floor, ground plane, cast shadow, frame, or scene.
- Genuine RGBA output with alpha=0 outside the weapon; no baked checkerboard.

Generation method
- Built-in image generation mode.
- The original generic ice-gun concept was rejected and replaced after user feedback.
- Left view generated as the redesigned canonical anchor; right/front derived by identity-preserving rotations; rear regenerated to obtain a real transparent alpha channel while retaining the same large forms and palette.
- RGB checkerboard results were rejected and reprocessed through background extraction until the selected files contained real alpha.
