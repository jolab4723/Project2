Create one realistic, game-ready Project2 sci-fi combat shotgun with a clean, broad, readable silhouette. Use dark-crimson heat-treated armor panels, satin tungsten barrel and fore-end surfaces, and restrained black joints. The receiver carries one flat, sharply bounded recessed forge-core. Gold-red emission is allowed only on that forge-core; do not reuse its exact color on normal paint, do not make the muzzle glow, and add no white or other glow. Keep the stock, receiver, twin barrel structure, trigger assembly, fore-end, and broad muzzle physically connected. Avoid excessive industrial bulges, greebles, rails, fins, accessories, or floating effects.

- 2048 x 1024 PNG RGBA with a genuine alpha-0 background.
- One complete centered horizontal weapon, strict orthographic projection, fully visible and uncropped.
- LEFT SIDE 90 degrees first, muzzle right. FRONT 0 degrees is the only view that looks into the broad open muzzle. REAR 180 degrees looks squarely at the buttpad. RIGHT SIDE 270 degrees uses the approved left view's exact horizontal mirror to guarantee scale and structural identity, with muzzle left.
- No hands, character, stand, floor, shadow, text, label, logo, watermark, aura, particles, detached parts, or background.

- The muzzle is broad, deep, mechanically continuous, centered, and unobstructed.
