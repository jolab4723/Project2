Design anchor
- Rare fire grenade launcher with an unmistakable colorful pyrotechnic/festival-launcher identity; a large-caliber launcher rather than a generic real rifle.
- One connected, durable, Meshy-friendly silhouette with a sturdy compact stock, pistol grip, connected foregrip, thick launch chamber, and an integrated flared starburst mortar muzzle.
- Lacquered crimson and deep navy body, brass/gold reinforcement, restrained cyan/magenta festival bands, and one sealed orange ember window.
- Solid radial burst embossing and reinforced rings only; no flags, ribbons, sparks, flames, loose ammunition, wires, charms, floating or fragile pieces.

View contract
- left.png: true orthographic left elevation, stock left / muzzle right.
- right.png: true orthographic opposite side, muzzle left / stock right.
- front.png: true orthographic muzzle view, camera centered on the barrel axis.
- rear.png: true orthographic stock view, camera centered on the butt pad.
- Identical major geometry, proportions, colors, material zones, and starburst identity across all views.
- Exactly one centered, fully visible weapon; no character, hands, text, label, logo, floor, ground plane, cast shadow, frame, or scene.
- Genuine RGBA output with alpha=0 outside the weapon; no baked checkerboard.

Generation method
- Built-in image generation mode.
- Left view generated as the canonical anchor; right/front/rear derived by identity-preserving rotations.
- RGB checkerboard results were rejected and reprocessed through background extraction until the selected files contained real alpha.
