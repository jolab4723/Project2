BASE DESIGN PROMPT

Use a clean, slim, readable construction with a practical stock, coherent receiver, straight barrel and fore-end, distinct trigger opening, and a clear open muzzle. Use dark jade-green anodized metal and matte black technical polymer only; do not force white accents.
