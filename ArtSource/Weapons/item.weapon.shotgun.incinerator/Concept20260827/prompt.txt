Item: item.weapon.shotgun.incinerator — Unique "Incinerator"

Canonical design
A bold, heavy, reconstruction-friendly incendiary shotgun. One robust connected silhouette: compact heavy stock and pistol grip, thick rectangular receiver, paired large connected cylindrical fire chambers integrated around a short vertically stacked double muzzle, and broad vented ceramic heat shields wrapping the forward half. Use a few large coherent forms, not ornate fantasy detail. Materials are scorched dark gunmetal, dark graphite, heat-stained brown steel, matte pale/charcoal heat-resistant ceramic, and dark rubber. Controlled deep-orange emission appears only through broad chamber windows and thick vent slits.

Shared render constraints
Polished stylized 3D game-asset concept render. Strict orthographic projection with zero perspective or foreshortening. One view per PNG, centered and fully unclipped with generous margin. Genuine alpha=0 transparent background; no checkerboard pixels, floor, shadow, pedestal, environment, hands, characters, text, labels, logos, annotations, measurement lines, flames, smoke, sparks, particles, or extra objects. No excessive greebles, fragile pipes, tiny wires, floating tanks, or hair-thin parts.
