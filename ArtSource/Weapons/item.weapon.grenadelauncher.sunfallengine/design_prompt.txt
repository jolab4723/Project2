Create one realistic, game-ready Project2 sci-fi grenade launcher with a large but simple, single-piece silhouette. Use obsidian-black ceramic/metal, dark gunmetal mechanisms, and restrained aged-brass structure. The receiver carries one sharply bounded recessed circular sun-core. Keep the stock, receiver, barrel, trigger assembly, fore-end, and broad muzzle physically connected. Avoid excessive industrial bulges, greebles, rails, fins, accessories, or floating effects.
